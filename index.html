THE PROBLEM

Pain is the last thing to show up.

By the time something hurts, your spine has usually been compensating for months. Whether you're dealing with nagging back pain, neck stiffness, sciatica, or chronic postural fatigue, we don't just chase the flare-up — we find what's driving it and restore proper structural movement.

Spinal & Disc Strain

Sciatica, acute or nagging low back pain, and deep spinal tension.

Postural & Neck Mobility

Desk-work tension, neck and shoulder stiffness, and movement compensation.

Functional Stability

Restoring proper structural movement so minor imbalances don't become setbacks.
